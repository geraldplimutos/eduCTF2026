While digging through the files on the memory card, you find one more file that wasn't there before. 

It’s an image that appears to be a screenshot from the Lab 2 CCTV camera.

The image is heavily corrupted—covered in black pixels and noise.

There are no people visible in the frame, just the empty chairs of the ComLab.

"The timestamp says May 3, 2026," Denmar observes. "That's right around the time the incident happened. Why is it so glitchy? It’s like someone tried to wipe the footage but didn't finish the job."

Even though the image is a mess, the file structure is still intact. In the world of digital forensics, even a "broken" photo can hide secrets in its metadata. If the person who messed with the camera left a trace, it’ll be buried in the image's internal notes.
